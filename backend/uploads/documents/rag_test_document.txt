SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW
===============================================

Zylotech Dynamics was founded in 2019 by CEO Renata Okafor and CTO
Marcus Feldt. The company is headquartered in Bristlecone, Nevada,
and operates three regional offices: Port Haldane, Wexbury, and
Calder's Ridge.

The company's core product is the "Sable-9 Controller," a
industrial automation unit released in March 2024.

SECTION 2: EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
===============================================

calendar year.

Rule 2.2 — Unused leave days may be carried over, but no more than
9 days may roll into the following year.

business days in advance, except for medical emergencies.

Rule 2.4 — The mandatory company-wide shutdown period is between
December 22 and January 2.

SECTION 4: EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
===============================================

In the event of a critical system fault, staff must follow this
exact sequence:

Step 1 — Trigger the isolation relay using override code ZX-7741-QT.
Step 2 — Notify the on-call engineer via the internal pager system
         (extension 8842).
Step 3 — Log the incident under classification tier "Amber-3" in
         the incident tracker.
Step 4 — Do not restart the Sable-9 unit until Facilities confirms
         ambient temperature is below 41°C.

The designated fallback safety officer for the Wexbury office is
Priya Ramaswamy. Her backup is Devon Achterberg.
